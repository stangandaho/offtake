# offtake: Sustainability Indices and Models for Wildlife Harvest and Hunting

Toolkit for assessing the sustainability of wildlife exploitation
(bushmeat hunting, recreational hunting and fisheries). It implements
the main index-based and model-based approaches catalogued by Adounke et
al. (2026)
[doi:10.1016/j.biocon.2025.111606](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.biocon.2025.111606)
and reviewed by Weinbaum et al. (2013)
[doi:10.1111/ele.12008](https://doi.org/10.1111/ele.12008) . Indices
compare a harvest or abundance metric between hunted and reference
sites: age structure comparison ('asc'), hunting yield comparison
('hyco') and population density comparison ('pdc'). Models compare
observed offtake with an estimated maximum sustainable harvest: the
production model of Robinson and Redford (1991) ('pro'), potential
biological removal of Wade (1998)
[doi:10.1111/j.1748-7692.1998.tb00688.x](https://doi.org/10.1111/j.1748-7692.1998.tb00688.x)
('pbr'), the maximum sustainable yield of the logistic model ('msy'),
the spatially explicit source-sink model of Levi et al. (2009) ('biode')
and a stochastic extension of PBR in the spirit of Manlik et al. (2022)
[doi:10.1111/cobi.13897](https://doi.org/10.1111/cobi.13897) ('samse').
